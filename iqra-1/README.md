IQRA 1 ADVENTURE — DIIN ISLAAM

This course is designed to sit at /iqra-1/ in the fes-reshid/barnoota GitHub Pages repository. It uses the existing shared KidsCloud authentication, so learners sign in with their current Tuhfatul Atfaal / Seeraa Quest / Qur'an Tracker username and password. Iqra course progress is saved separately under the `iqra-1` game key.

The learning journey has 16 map tabs, each with a winding road and five lesson stops. Maps and lesson stops unlock as the student completes the previous map/lesson. The look is a bright, Reading Eggs-style kids' game: a sky-and-hills background, the rounded Fredoka font (Amiri for Arabic letters), chunky 3D buttons, colourful letter tiles, an owl mascot that cheers, confetti and bouncing stars. Every tap makes a soft pop; right answers, mistakes and finished lessons have their own sounds, and a 🔔 Sounds button in the header mutes them (remembered per device).

Tapping a stop opens a Tuhfatul-style lesson screen: a colourful side panel (map/lesson, coins, home/sound/restart tools and a step list with ticks) and one activity at a time with a step counter and progress bar. Lessons 1–4 go Meet (each letter one by one) → Draw (trace each letter over a grey guide with a finger; the drawing is checked against the letter shape and earns 1–3 stars) → Match them all → Letter Detective → Reward. Lesson 5 goes Listen to every letter → Repeat → Match them all (in rounds of up to six) → Reward. Each lesson's letters come from the map's `focus` list (for example Map 1 Lesson 2 is just Alif). Teachers signed in through the Teacher button can open any lesson to preview it.

The Teacher button uses an existing approved teacher/admin account. It opens a teacher-only lesson editor and audio studio with two tabs: "Lesson sounds" (record/upload one clip per lesson stop, as before) and "Letter sounds" (record or upload a pronunciation clip for each of the 28 Arabic letters, plus each letter combined with Fatha, one at a time). A third tab, "Game sounds", lets the teacher record or upload their own voice for four reactions (Welcome, Correct answer, Try again, Lesson complete); a recorded clip replaces the built-in chime for every student, and "Use chime" switches back. In teacher mode each letter card in a lesson also has a 🎙️ Record my voice button, and a 🎙️ side button opens the studio on the lesson being viewed. Clips are saved in the shared KidsCloud course content and load for signed-in learners.

Letters in the lessons are tappable by students and play the teacher's recorded sound for that letter (Meet, Draw, Match, Letter Detective and Listen all have 🔊 or tappable letters). If nothing has been recorded yet, the 🔊 buttons show a gentle reminder to ask the teacher.

The map titles follow Iqra Book 1 pages 1–30 based on the referenced page-by-page guide, plus pages 33–34 for Map 16, which introduces the Fatha vowel mark (a short "a" sound) on every letter. Lesson text is editable by teachers.

Follow the Best Example

﴿لَقَدْ كَانَ لَكُمْ فِي رَسُولِ اللَّهِ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ لِمَنْ كَانَ يَرْجُو اللَّهَ وَالْيَوْمَ الْآخِرَ وَذَكَرَ اللَّهَ كَثِيرًا﴾

“Indeed, in the Messenger of Allah you have an excellent example for whoever has hope in Allah and the Last Day, and remembers Allah often.”

Sūrah al-Aḥzāb 33:21

﴿وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٍ﴾

“And indeed, you are truly a man of outstanding character.”

Sūrah al-Qalam 68:4

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was truthful, trustworthy, patient and merciful. Allah sent him as a mercy and made his noble character a beautiful example for us. In Seeraa Adventure, you will learn his story, love him more, and discover how to follow his Sunnah in your daily life. 🌟

1

Sign in and join your class

Open diinislaam.com/seeraa and sign in with your student username and password. Your progress is saved to your account so you can continue your journey later.

  1. Enter your student username and password.
  2. Open the ☰ menu on your map.
  3. Select Join your class, enter the six-character class code from your teacher, and tap Join class.
💡 Keep your login details private. Ask your teacher for help if you cannot sign in.
2

Explore the 45-map Seeraa journey

Seeraa Adventure follows the life of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ in historical order, using content aligned with Ar-Raheeq al-Makhtum (The Sealed Nectar). The course contains 45 maps and 180 lessons—four lessons on every map.

  • 🏜️ Begin with Arabia before Islam and the world into which the Prophet ﷺ was born.
  • 🕋 Travel through the Makkah period, the first revelation and the early Muslims.
  • 🐪 Follow the Hijrah and the building of the Muslim community in Madinah.
  • 🛡️ Study the major events, treaties and expeditions with age-appropriate explanations.
  • 🌙 Finish with the final years, the Prophet’s ﷺ character and his lasting legacy.

Tap the glowing lesson marker to begin. When a lesson is complete, your explorer moves along the road. You can revisit completed lessons whenever you want.

ℹ️ Use About this place to understand the historical setting. Use Pause animations when you want a quieter map.

A Mercy to Every World

﴿وَمَا أَرْسَلْنَاكَ إِلَّا رَحْمَةً لِّلْعَالَمِينَ﴾

“We have not sent you, O Prophet, except as a mercy to the worlds.”

Sūrah al-Anbiyāʾ 21:107

Look for the Prophet’s ﷺ mercy—to children, families, neighbours, companions, strangers and even those who opposed him.

3

Complete a Seeraa lesson

Every lesson teaches one part of the Prophetic biography through an age-appropriate story, Islamic evidence, vocabulary, activities and questions.

  1. 📖 Read the story — notice the people, place and order of events.
  2. 🌟 Learn — study the key facts, benefits and connected Qur’an verse or authentic narration.
  3. 🔤 Explore words — learn useful Arabic names and Seeraa vocabulary with English meanings.
  4. 🗣️ Remember and retell — explain the event in your own words.
  5. 🃏 Review cards — revise people, places, events and important meanings.
  6. 🧩 Build the timeline — arrange events in the correct order.
  7. ✅ Check your understanding — answer multiple-choice and true-or-false questions.
🔊 Use sound buttons to hear Arabic titles. If your teacher adds a lesson video, watch it before continuing.
4

Finish the map challenge

After completing all four lessons, review the map and open the 15-question Map Challenge. It checks people, places, important facts, the order of events, Islamic evidence and lessons for daily life.

  • Review the map cards before starting.
  • Read every question carefully and do not rush.
  • Use ← Previous and Next → to go back, check and change any answer. Answers are marked only when you send the final reflection — then Check your answers shows every question.
  • Score 12 out of 15 or higher to pass.
  • Your best result is saved, and you can revise and try again.
  • Write the final reflection in your own words and send it to your teacher when requested.
5

Teacher review and the next map

At the end of a map, your teacher may ask you to retell the main events, explain what you learned or discuss the character lesson. This checks your understanding of the Seeraa.

  1. Finish the four lessons and map challenge.
  2. Open Teacher check and message your teacher when you are ready.
  3. Your teacher reviews your understanding and gives feedback.
  4. When the map is approved, continue through the exit to the next historical stage.
A teacher may ask you to revisit a lesson or improve an answer. This helps you learn the Seeraa accurately.
6

Homework, files and messages

Open the ☰ menu and use the teacher area to stay connected with your class.

  • 📚 Homework — read the task, check the due date and complete it.
  • 📎 Upload work — attach a PDF, Word document or PowerPoint when requested.
  • 📁 Class files — download worksheets and Seeraa resources from your teacher.
  • 🎬 Teacher videos — watch linked lesson videos inside the course.
  • ✉️ Messages — ask questions and read private feedback.
  • 📢 Announcements and quizzes — check new class updates and activities.
📎 Check that you selected the correct file before uploading it. Teacher feedback appears with your submitted work.
7

Progress, rewards and review

  • 🔥 Streak — practise on consecutive days.
  • 🪙 Coins and stars — earn rewards by completing lessons and challenges.
  • 📍 Lessons completed — track your journey through all 180 lessons.
  • 🏅 Badges — unlock achievements for effort and milestones.
  • 🌟 Leading learners — view the class board when your teacher enables it.
  • 🎭 Explorer — personalise your avatar and watch it travel across the maps.
  • 🔁 Daily review — revisit earlier stories and questions so you remember them.

Rewards make learning enjoyable, but the main goal is to know Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, love his Sunnah and improve your own character.

Noble Character

﴿وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٍ﴾

“And indeed, you are truly a man of outstanding character.”

Sūrah al-Qalam 68:4

Every Seeraa story should help us practise a beautiful quality such as truthfulness, patience, mercy, courage, forgiveness or trust in Allah.

8

Learn Seeraa successfully

  • Complete one lesson carefully instead of rushing through many.
  • Say the names of people and places aloud to remember them.
  • Tell a parent, sibling or teacher what happened after each lesson.
  • Keep the sequence clear: before Prophethood → Makkah → Hijrah → Madinah → final years.
  • Choose one character lesson to practise in your daily life.
  • Ask your teacher whenever something is unclear.
🤲 Ask Allah to increase you in beneficial knowledge and help you follow the beautiful example of His Messenger ﷺ.

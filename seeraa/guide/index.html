Follow the Best Example

﴿لَقَدْ كَانَ لَكُمْ فِي رَسُولِ اللَّهِ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ لِمَنْ كَانَ يَرْجُو اللَّهَ وَالْيَوْمَ الْآخِرَ وَذَكَرَ اللَّهَ كَثِيرًا﴾

“Indeed, in the Messenger of Allah you have an excellent example for whoever has hope in Allah and the Last Day, and remembers Allah often.”

Sūrah al-Aḥzāb 33:21

﴿وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٍ﴾

“And indeed, you are truly a man of outstanding character.”

Sūrah al-Qalam 68:4

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was truthful, trustworthy, patient and merciful. Allah sent him as a mercy and made his noble character a beautiful example for us. In Seeraa Adventure, you will learn his story, love him more, and discover how to follow his Sunnah in your daily life. 🌟

1

Sign in or create your account

Open diinislaam.com/seeraa. If you are new, tap Create account, choose a username (for example amina123) and a password with at least 6 letters or numbers. Next time just tap Sign in.

💡 Write your username down and keep it safe. Your teacher and parents use it to find you. The same account works for Seeraa Quest and the Qur’an Tracker.
2

Join your class

  1. Ask your teacher for your class code — 6 letters, like TQ7K2M.
  2. On your map, tap ☰ in the top-right corner. In the menu, find “From your teacher” and tap 🏫 Join your class.
  3. Type the code and tap Join class. ✅ Now you see your class news, homework, quizzes and the class leaderboard.
📎 If your homework says “Hand in your work”, tap 📎 Upload my file and choose your PDF, Word or PowerPoint file (up to 5 MB). You can ↻ Replace it or 🗑 Remove it, and your teacher’s feedback appears right under it. 💬
🎬 If your teacher added a video to your homework, open 📚 Homework and tap 🎬 Watch the video — it plays right there. Tap ✕ Close the video when you finish.
3

Your map — start here

The Tuhfah is split into 15 maps. Each map is a road with a few lines of the poem — and every map is a different place: a sunny meadow, rain, mountains, desert, snow, the seaside, a starry night and more. Your character stands next to the first lesson and points to it — “Start here!”. Tap the glowing circle to open the lesson.

The map fills your whole screen. Tap ☰ in the top-right corner to open the menu. At the top you see your name and scores. Then:

  • 🗺️ My maps, 📝 From your teacher, 👩‍🏫 Teacher check and 🔁 Daily review open in a box over the map. Tap ✕ to go back to the map.
  • 🌟 Leading learners shows who is leading in your class (by lessons, then stars). It shows only when your teacher has switched it on.
  • 📘 Course book downloads the book to print and follow along.
  • The buttons under Practise (Memorise, Tajweed rules, badges, avatar, words and flashcards) open their own pages.
  • At the bottom: sound, ⛶ Full screen (on a computer or tablet), the guide and Sign out.

The bar at the top of the menu shows how you are doing:

  • 🔥 Streak — days in a row you practised. Come back every day to keep it burning!
  • 🪙 Coins and ⭐ stars — earned in lessons and challenges.
  • 🧠 Memorised lines and 📍 lessons done out of 62.
  • Tap your character to change how they look — clothes, colours and hijab or turban.

The best of people

«خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ»

“The best of you are those who learn the Qur’an and teach it.”

The Prophet ﷺ · Sahih al-Bukhari 5027

When you learn Tajweed today, you can teach your family and friends tomorrow — inshā’ Allāh!

4

A lesson in 7 steps

Every lesson teaches one line of the Tuhfah. Follow the steps on the left, from top to bottom:

  1. 🎧 Matn — listen to the line and read along.
  2. 📖 Learn — what the line means and the Tajweed rule, with examples from the Qur’an.
  3. 🔤 Words — match each Arabic word with its meaning.
  4. 🗣️ Memorise — the same 5 levels as Memorise mode (read along, half hidden, first letters, from memory, record yourself). Pass level 4 to open the next step — recording yourself is optional.
  5. 🃏 Flash Card — quick revision.
  6. 🧩 Build — put the words of the line in the right order.
  7. 🏆 Five Questions — a short quiz to finish the lesson.
✓ Steps you finish get a tick and stay open — you can go back and revise any time without waiting. If you finish a lesson again, it counts as a new attempt.
5

Walk along the road

When you finish a lesson, your character walks to the next lesson on the road. After 15 seconds the next lesson opens by itself — or tap Stay on map to rest.

6

Practice Cards & the 15-question challenge

After the last lesson of a map, two new stops open at the end of the road:

  • 🃏 Practice Cards — flip every card to revise the whole map.
  • 🏆 15 Questions — the Map Challenge. Score 12 or more to pass.

✍️ Question 15 is your reflection on the map, in four parts: 📖 what I learned, 🌱 what I will do, 🤲 the duʿāʾ I learned (each map shows one to learn), and ✅ have I practised it? Write in your own words and tap 📨 Send to my teacher — it goes to your teacher like a message. Sending it earns the 15th point, and then you see your result. Your teacher answers under the map in From your teacher and in your messages — with a note, corrections, or a request to ✍️ Write it again.

💡 Didn’t pass yet? No problem — review the lessons and tap Try Again ↻. Your best score is always saved.

Ten rewards for every letter

«مَنْ قَرَأَ حَرْفًا مِنْ كِتَابِ اللَّهِ فَلَهُ بِهِ حَسَنَةٌ، وَالْحَسَنَةُ بِعَشْرِ أَمْثَالِهَا»

“Whoever reads a letter from the Book of Allah gets a good deed, and each good deed is multiplied ten times.”

The Prophet ﷺ · Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 2910

Every line you practise is full of rewards — keep going!

7

Teacher check & the door to the next map

Every map has an exit door at the end of the road (top right). If you tap it before you finish the map, it tells you what is left — lessons, Practice Cards or the 15-question quiz. When you pass the challenge, your character stands by the door and points to it. Tap the door:

  1. Recite all the lines of the map to your teacher (Talqeen).
  2. Open ☰ → Teacher check and tap ✉️ Message my teacher — or call your teacher — and ask them to open the door for you.
  3. The door opens! Tap it — your character walks through and into the next map. 🎉

To go back, tap the door at the bottom left (it says ◀ Map 1, ◀ Map 2 …). It opens and takes you to the map before.

🏅 After the teacher signs, you can also open your certificate for that map.
8

Memorise in 5 levels

Open the ☰ menu and tap 🧠 Memorise. You see every line up to your map — your map is open, earlier maps are folded (tap Map 1 ▸ to open them for revision).

👀1. Read along
🌗2. Half hidden
🔤3. First letters
🧠4. From memory
🎙️5. Record & listen (optional)
  • Press ▶ Whole line and choose how many repeats (1×, 3×, 5×, 10×). Keep “Pause for me to repeat” on so you can say it after the reciter.
  • When you can say it at that level, tap ✓ I can say it at this level. Reach level 4 to earn ⭐ and 🪙 5 coins!
  • Level 5 (record yourself) is optional. After level 4 you can tap Skip recording — next line → and go on.

Read and rise

«يُقَالُ لِصَاحِبِ الْقُرْآنِ: اقْرَأْ وَارْتَقِ وَرَتِّلْ كَمَا كُنْتَ تُرَتِّلُ فِي الدُّنْيَا، فَإِنَّ مَنْزِلَكَ عِنْدَ آخِرِ آيَةٍ تَقْرَؤُهَا»

“It will be said to the companion of the Qur’an: Read, rise up, and recite as you used to recite in the world — your place will be at the last ayah you recite.”

The Prophet ﷺ · Sunan Abī Dāwūd 1464 · Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 2914

Just like you climb from map to map here, the people of the Qur’an climb in Jannah. Memorise a little every day!

9

Record yourself & send it to your teacher

In Memorise, open 🎙️ 5. Record & listen:

  1. Tap 🎙️ Record myself and say the whole line from memory. Tap ■ Stop.
  2. Press ▶ and listen to yourself — does it sound right?
  3. Tap 📤 Send to my teacher. When your teacher listens, you will see their feedback right under your recording. 💬
  • 🟢 WhatsApp or ✉️ Email — send it to yourself or your family.
  • ⬇️ Download — keep the audio file.
  • 🗑 Delete — remove a recording you don’t want.
🔒 Recordings stay on your device until you send, share or download them. Your parents can see your teacher’s feedback in their parent report.
10

Class files — download worksheets

Your teacher can share PDF worksheets and printables with your class. Find them under the map in “From your teacher” → 📁 Class files. A New tag shows when your teacher adds a file.

  • The number shows how many files your teacher shared.
  • On Map 1 you also find the Week 1 Whole-Week Practice Pack — seven pages to practise at home.
  • New files appear by themselves — no need to reload.
11

Tajweed rules, badges & messages

You find all of these in the ☰ menu.

  • 📖 Tajweed rules — every rule of the Tuhfah with its letters and examples. Tap an example to hear it.
  • 🏅 Badges — collect them for streaks, finished maps, memorised lines and more.
  • ✉️ Messages with your teacher — ask a question or say salām. You can edit a message if you made a mistake.

Your friend on the Day of Judgement

«اقْرَءُوا الْقُرْآنَ فَإِنَّهُ يَأْتِي يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ شَفِيعًا لِأَصْحَابِهِ»

“Read the Qur’an, for it will come on the Day of Resurrection as an intercessor for its companions.”

The Prophet ﷺ · Sahih Muslim 804

«الْمَاهِرُ بِالْقُرْآنِ مَعَ السَّفَرَةِ الْكِرَامِ الْبَرَرَةِ، وَالَّذِي يَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ وَيَتَتَعْتَعُ فِيهِ وَهُوَ عَلَيْهِ شَاقٌّ لَهُ أَجْرَانِ»

“The one skilled in the Qur’an is with the noble, righteous angels, and the one who stumbles over it, finding it hard, gets two rewards.”

The Prophet ﷺ · Sahih Muslim 798

Finding it hard? You get two rewards! A little every day — 10 minutes is enough. May Allah make the Qur’an the light of your heart. 🤲

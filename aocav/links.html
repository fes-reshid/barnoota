Useful links

The services Oromo families in Australia ask us about most — interpreters, Centrelink, visas, English classes, health, housing, schools and legal help — plus Afaan Oromoo media. Everything here is free to contact unless it says otherwise.

Ask for an interpreter. Every government office, hospital and doctor in Australia can call a free Afaan Oromoo interpreter for you. You do not have to bring a friend or your children to translate — just say “I need an Oromo interpreter.”

Links are checked by our committee but government pages do move. If something here is broken or out of date, please tell us and we will fix it. AOCAV is not affiliated with these organisations and does not control what they publish.

Not sure where to start?

Come to our help desk in Footscray on a Thursday. Someone who speaks Afaan Oromoo will sit with you, read the letter, and make the call with you.

If you need help now

We are a community group, not an emergency service

If you or someone you love is in danger, please call these numbers first. Then call us — we will walk beside you afterwards.

Emergency
000 — police, fire, ambulance
Interpreter
131 450 — TIS National, free telephone interpreting in Afaan Oromoo
Crisis support
13 11 14 — Lifeline, 24 hours
Family violence
1800 015 188 — Safe Steps Victoria, 24 hours
Health advice
1300 60 60 24 — NURSE-ON-CALL Victoria, 24 hours

These are Australian public services, listed here for convenience. AOCAV is not affiliated with them.

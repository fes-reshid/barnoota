Who we are

A community association built by Oromo families, for Oromo families.

AOCAV was founded in 1984 by Oromo people who had made Victoria their home and wanted somewhere their children could hear their language, their elders could be honoured, and newly arrived families would never have to work things out alone.

Four decades later that is still exactly what we do. We are a not-for-profit incorporated association and a registered charity, run by our own members — volunteers who give their Saturdays to teach, drive, cook, translate, mentor and organise.

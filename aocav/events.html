Elders Voices

Connecting generations · Preserving culture · Strengthening community.

A welcoming afternoon where our elders sit with young people over a cup of coffee and tell the stories that don't get written down — life in Oromia, the journey to Australia, the first years in Melbourne, what they want the next generation to carry forward.

Elders, families and young people are all welcome. There is no cost, no formality and no need to book, though it helps us with the catering if you let us know you're coming.

  • Saturday 17 October 2026, 2:00pm – 5:00pm
  • Oromo Resource Centre
    664–678 Downing St, Mount Cottrell VIC 3024
  • Free · Coffee ceremony and refreshments provided

Organised by the Australian Oromo Community Association in Victoria. Funded by the City of Melbourne.

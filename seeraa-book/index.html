An illustrated series

Seeraa Quest

The life of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, from a valley full of idols to the day the gates opened — in comic pages, with room to stop, think and draw.

How these books are drawn

Following the practice of Islamic publishing, the Prophet ﷺ and his Companions are never pictured. The storytelling is carried by light, by silhouette and by landscape — and where his words appear, they appear as words.

The history follows Ar-Raheeq al-Makhtum (The Sealed Nectar), in order, from the age before Islam to the final years.

How to read them

Turn the pages with the arrows, the arrow keys, or a swipe. Chapters opens the contents. Your place is kept, so you can stop anywhere and come back to it.

Pause and Think pages have room to write. Your Turn to Draw pages have an empty panel and a few tips from the artist. Use Print to put the whole book on paper, drawing pages and all.

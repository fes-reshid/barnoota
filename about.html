About Diin Islaam

A growing home for clear, engaging and accessible Islamic learning.

Our purpose

Diin Islaam is an independent educational project based in Melbourne, Australia. It brings together free Islamic courses, learning games and practical apps for adults, children, parents and teachers.

Our aim is simple: make beneficial learning easier to begin, easier to understand and easier to continue. The website supports English, Arabic and Afaan Oromoo learners and is designed to work well on phones as well as computers.

Structured learningLessons are organised into courses, maps and activities so learners can see what comes next.
Active practiceQuizzes, flash cards, listening and repetition help turn reading into real learning.
Family friendlyChildren's areas are designed for use with parent, guardian or teacher supervision.
Always improvingWe welcome corrections and suggestions when a lesson, translation or technical feature needs attention.

Educational approach

We combine short explanations with practice. Depending on the course, a learner may read a story, listen to audio, review vocabulary, answer questions and repeat key material. Some classroom features allow teachers to guide progress.

Sources and religious guidance

We aim to use recognised Islamic sources and to identify Qur'an and hadith references where they appear. The site is an educational aid and is not a replacement for a qualified scholar or teacher. For a personal ruling or a matter requiring scholarly judgement, please ask a trusted qualified scholar.

Corrections and feedback

If you find a content, translation, accessibility or technical issue, please send the page link and a short explanation through our Contact page. We will review it as the project continues to grow.

Our commitment: useful learning, clear navigation, respectful content and transparent information about privacy and advertising.

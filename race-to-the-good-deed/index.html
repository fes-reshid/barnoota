How to Play

  • ✅ Exactly four players race together, each with a fixed character: Player 1 = Zayd (Blue), Player 2 = Layla (Orange), Player 3 = Malik (Red), Player 4 = Amira (Pink).
  • ✅ Controls (hold Right to run): Zayd D/W/S/A · Layla Arrow Keys · Malik L/I/K/J · Amira H/T/G/F -- each player's four keys are Right(move) / Up(jump) / Down(slide) / Left(ability).
  • ✅ On a phone or tablet, each player gets RUN/JUMP/ABILITY buttons in their own corner of the screen (Slide is keyboard-only). Landscape orientation on a tablet works best for four players at once.
  • ✅ Jump again in the air for a double jump. Hold Down while running to slide.
  • ✅ Each character's ability is unique: Zayd bursts faster, Layla flies over gaps, Malik smashes rocks, Amira activates switches -- and once a switch is opened, it stays open for everyone.
  • ✅ Reaching the Good Deed first starts a quick good-choice question for everyone to think about.
  • ✅ There's no fighting and no "worst player" -- results are shown as Speed, Kindness and Teamwork stars, and everyone gets positive feedback.

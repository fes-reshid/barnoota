## Description (paste into KDP; it accepts basic HTML)

<b>Trace the journey from Makkah to Madinah, one clue at a time.</b>

<p>Inspired by the blessed journey of the Hijrah, <b>Hijrah Route</b> is a book of 200 logic puzzles for adults. Each puzzle asks you to find one unbroken route from Makkah to Madinah using number clues, a few pre-drawn pieces, and pure logic.</p>

<p>Every puzzle has number clues for each row and column, telling you how many squares are on the route. Work out which squares belong, join them into one continuous path, and enjoy the satisfaction of seeing the route complete.</p>

<p><b>Every puzzle has exactly one solution.</b> All puzzles were checked by computer, so there is never any guessing.</p>

<p><b>Inside this book:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>100 short-route puzzles on 6x6 grids and 100 long-route puzzles on 8x8 grids</li>
<li>Puzzles that go from easy to harder, so you can build your skills</li>
<li>A short page on the Hijrah and clear instructions with a worked example</li>
<li>One puzzle per page, with room to pencil in your ideas</li>
<li>Full solutions at the back</li>
<li>Portable 6 x 9 inch size, easy to take anywhere</li>
</ul>

<p>A calm, screen-free pastime for a quiet evening, a coffee break, or a long journey. All you need is a pencil.</p>

<p>The puzzles are a game inspired by the Hijrah and do not show the real route.</p>

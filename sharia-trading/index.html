Start with the essentials

What is an ETF?

An exchange-traded fund holds a collection of investments. You buy units in the fund on an exchange, similar to shares. Diversification can reduce single-company exposure, but funds can still lose money.

What does Sharia-screened mean?

A screening provider evaluates business activities and financial criteria under a particular methodology. Methods differ, holdings change, and some income may require purification. Check the issuer’s current documents and consult a qualified scholar. This website is not a certification body.

Can profit be guaranteed?

No stock-trading bot or equity ETF can guarantee profit. A stop-loss or a buy pause does not guarantee a maximum loss. Prices may gap, and real orders may execute at worse prices.

What is missing from this demo?

Live prices, a broker, independent stock screening, background operation, account login and cloud synchronisation are not connected. Dividends, tax, fund expenses, market spreads and variable exchange rates are not modelled. Demo fees are illustrative, not any broker’s tariff.

Does the replay prove profitability?

No. It is a scripted demonstration using fictional prices. A future strategy requires genuine historical data, out-of-sample testing, realistic costs and a matched buy-and-hold benchmark. A few profitable months do not prove it will work in the future.

A careful path forward

01 · Understand

Explore the fund documents and learn the difference between a share, an ETF and a screening result.

02 · Practise

Use virtual money. Observe both falling and rising prices and the effect of costs.

03 · Connect verified data

Choose an appropriate licensed data source, fresh screening evidence and a broker before building real-market paper trading.

04 · Review independently

Discuss suitability with a licensed financial adviser and Sharia requirements with a qualified scholar. Real trading requires a separate, deliberate setup.

Privacy

Last updated: October 2026. This page covers this website and the Noorsoft apps (NoorCleaner and NoorGuard).

The apps

  • The apps need no account and work on your PC without the internet. Your files are never uploaded.
  • A few options use the internet only when you switch them on:
    • NoorGuard, country of an attacker: sends only the attacker's public internet address to the service ipwho.is. Your own addresses, files and name are never sent. Off by default.
    • NoorGuard, password check: sends only the first 5 characters of a fingerprint (SHA-1 hash) of the password you type to the Have I Been Pwned service, which never learns the password. Used only when you press Check.
    • Windows tools: buttons that open Windows Update, the Microsoft Store (for Windows File Recovery) or Microsoft Defender use Microsoft's services under Microsoft's own terms.
  • The apps save their settings, attack history and quarantined files on your PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\NoorCleaner. Uninstalling the app does not delete your quarantined files.

This website

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Installers are downloaded from GitHub Releases. GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address when you visit or download, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and may show ads from Google AdSense. Google may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads; see Google's policies at policies.google.com/technologies/ads, where you can also manage ad personalisation.
  • Your chosen language is remembered in your own browser (local storage). It is not sent to us.

          Guide & shortcuts

          Getting started

          • Open… loads one or more audio files; each gets its own tab. You can also drag files onto the page.
          • The + next to the tabs starts an empty file: record into it, paste audio copied from another tab, or drop a file on it.
          • Click the waveform to place the cursor, drag to select. Effects apply to the selection — or to the whole file when nothing is selected.

          Combining two recordings

          1. Open both files — they appear as tabs.
          2. In the first tab, select the part you want and press Ctrl+C.
          3. Switch tabs, click where it should go and press Ctrl+V to insert it, or Ctrl+Shift+V to play it on top of what's there.
          4. Mix with file… layers a whole tab or file at a chosen volume — e.g. a voice over quiet background audio.

          Splitting a recitation into lines

          1. Press Auto-split at pauses. A red marker is placed in the middle of every pause.
          2. Too many parts? Lower the threshold (e.g. −45 dB) or raise the minimum pause length. Too few? Do the opposite.
          3. Drag markers to adjust them, press M to add one, or ✕ in the list to remove one.
          4. Press ▶ next to a part to check it, then Download all parts for a zip of numbered files.

          Effects & tools

          • Effects with … open a small window: set the values, press ▶ Preview to hear up to 8 seconds, then Apply.
          • Noise reduction (like WavePad): select a second or two where there is only background noise — no voice — and press Grab noise sample. Then clear the selection (Esc) and press Noise reduction…; preview, and raise or lower the amount. Without a sample it guesses the noise from the quietest parts.
          • Clean up a recording: Remove rumble (hum and wind) → Noise reduction (steady hiss) → Noise gate (anything left between words) → Normalise.
          • Repeat loop repeats the selected line several times, with an optional pause — useful for memorising.
          • Auto trim… cuts the silence at the start and end, or shortens every long pause inside the recording to a length you choose — great for tightening a recitation or a lecture.
          • Split into tabs opens each marked part (or each side of the cursor) as a separate tab. Join audio… does the opposite: tick open tabs and/or add files from your computer, put them in order with ↑ ↓, choose a pause or a crossfade between them, and they're joined into a new tab (also on the start screen as Join audio files).
          • Batch convert processes many files at once — e.g. convert a folder of WAV recordings to normalised MP3s.
          • Pitch… makes a voice higher or lower while keeping the same speed (+12 semitones = one octave up). Tempo… makes it faster or slower while keeping the same voice — e.g. 80% to slow a recitation down for learning. Speed… changes both together, like a tape.
          • The playback speed menu (1×) only changes how you listen; use Tempo… or Speed… to change the file itself.
          • History lists every change; click any line to go back to that point.

          Saving

          • Choose MP3 (small, good for sharing) or WAV (lossless, bigger). 192 kbps MP3 is a good default.
          • A * on a tab means it has changes you haven't downloaded yet.
          • Everything stays on your device — nothing is uploaded — so download your work before closing the page.

Privacy Policy — Hisnul Muslim

Last updated: September 2026

Hisnul Muslim ("the app") is a free reference app for adhkar (daily supplications) with Arabic text and Afaan Oromoo translation. It's available both as a website and as a native Android app. This page explains what information the app does and does not handle.

Data we collect

The app itself does not have a server, does not require an account, and does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information directly. The website shows ads served by Google AdSense, and the native Android app shows ads served by Google AdMob — both may use cookies, advertising IDs, or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. See "Advertising" below for details and how to control this.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app remembers is stored only on your device, using your browser's local storage and offline cache:

  • Favorites — which chapters you've bookmarked
  • Settings — font size, light/dark theme
  • Tasbih counter — your current dhikr count and preset
  • Downloaded audio — audio files you explicitly chose to download for offline playback, cached locally so future playback doesn't need the internet

None of this ever leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Advertising

On the website, the app shows ads served by Google AdSense on some pages. In the native Android app, ads are instead served by Google AdMob — a separate Google product built for mobile apps; AdSense is never used inside the app itself. Both may use cookies, advertising IDs (such as your device's Android Advertising ID), or similar technologies to serve ads and measure their performance, and may use data about your visits or app usage to provide ads tailored to your interests.

You can learn more, and opt out of personalized advertising, at Google Ads Settings, manage ad personalization on your Android device under Settings > Privacy > Ads, and read more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-party audio

Recitation audio is streamed directly from archive.org and everyayah.com, two public, third-party audio archives. When you play or download audio, your device connects directly to those services to fetch the file. Their own privacy practices apply to that connection; we don't intermediate it or see it.

Permissions

On some devices the app offers to be added to your home screen ("Install app" / "Add to Home Screen"). This does not require or transmit any personal data.

Children's privacy

The app itself does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, and is not directed at children. Ads are served by Google AdSense (website) or Google AdMob (Android app) under Google's own policies for the app's audience.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page.

The best of people

«خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ»

“The best of you are those who learn the Qur’an and teach it.”

The Prophet ﷺ · Sahih al-Bukhari 5027

﴿وَلَٰكِن كُونُوا رَبَّانِيِّينَ بِمَا كُنتُمْ تُعَلِّمُونَ الْكِتَابَ وَبِمَا كُنتُمْ تَدْرُسُونَ﴾

“Rather, be devoted servants of the Lord, because of what you have taught of the Book and because of what you have studied.”

Sūrah Āl ʿImrān 3:79

Jazākum Allāhu khayran for teaching the children of this ummah. Every letter they read correctly because of you is written for you too. This guide shows every tool in the Teacher Portal, step by step.

1

Your first day

  1. Add your full name — tap ✍️ Add your full name (or My profile). It is printed on every certificate you sign.
  2. Create your class — Classes → type a name (e.g. “Saturday Girls 8–10”) → + Create class. You get a 6-letter class code.
  3. Bring students in — give children the code, or create logins for new children in Add students.
  4. Set the first homework, e.g. “Memorise line 1”, and take attendance in each lesson.
💡 Use the ☰ menu on the left to move between tools. On a phone it folds away — tap ☰ to open it.
2

Overview — what needs you today

Open the portal before class. The Overview shows your numbers and everything waiting for you:

  • ⏳ Needs your review — students who finished a map and passed its challenge. Tap Review →.
  • 🎙️ New recitations — recordings students sent you. Tap Listen →.
  • Messages and announcements with a number badge are waiting for your reply.
3

Classes & join codes

  • Students type the code in Tajweed Quest under From your teacher → 🏫 Join your class.
  • From a class you can message it, announce to it, set its homework and print a class report.
  • 🏆 Class leaderboard: switch it on to let the class see each other’s streak, lines memorised and stars — switch it off any time.
4

Students & the student card

Search by name, username or guardian, filter by class or status, and switch between List, Grid and Gallery views.

Tap a student to open their card: Profile · Progress · Maps & certificates · Quiz results · Talqeen · Account, plus 👪 Parent link, 🖨️ Report and ✉️ Message.

💬 In Maps you can write a personal note for one map and attach a personal quiz — it appears under that map for that child only.

Gatherings surrounded by angels

«وَمَا اجْتَمَعَ قَوْمٌ فِي بَيْتٍ مِنْ بُيُوتِ اللَّهِ يَتْلُونَ كِتَابَ اللَّهِ وَيَتَدَارَسُونَهُ بَيْنَهُمْ إِلَّا نَزَلَتْ عَلَيْهِمُ السَّكِينَةُ وَغَشِيَتْهُمُ الرَّحْمَةُ وَحَفَّتْهُمُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ وَذَكَرَهُمُ اللَّهُ فِيمَنْ عِنْدَهُ»

“No people gather in one of the houses of Allah, reciting the Book of Allah and studying it together, except that tranquillity descends upon them, mercy covers them, the angels surround them, and Allah mentions them to those with Him.”

The Prophet ﷺ · Sahih Muslim 2699

Your class is such a gathering. Make the children feel it — begin with Bismillāh and end with a duʿā’.

5

Approve a map & sign the certificate

  1. A student finishes every lesson of a map and scores 12/15 or more in its Map Challenge → they appear in Needs your review.
  2. Tap Review →. Listen to the student recite every line of that map (Talqeen).
  3. Tick Hifz completed and Good recitation, write feedback, sign with your finger or mouse, then Approve.
🚪 In the student’s app, a door at the end of the road says “Please ask your teacher to listen to you and open the door for you.” Your approval opens that door — the child walks into the next map and gets a certificate with your name.
6

Recitations & feedback

Students record themselves in Memorise → 5. Record & listen and tap 📤 Send to my teacher. Their recording arrives in Overview → New recitations and in the student’s 🎙️ Talqeen tab.

  • Your feedback appears under the child’s recording — and in the parent report.
  • Turn on Talqeen for this student to record lines for them to copy (up to 1 minute each).
7

Homework

  • Types: Memorise lines (done at Memorise level 4) · Finish lessons · Pass a quiz · Record lines · 📎 Hand in a file · Other task (the student ticks it).
  • 📎 Hand in a file: students upload a PDF, Word or PowerPoint file (up to 5 MB). Open ▾ See each student to download each file and write feedback — the student sees it under their file.
  • Choose the lines, a due date and the class — the title fills itself in.
  • 🔔 Remind sends a private message to everyone not done.
  • 🎬 YouTube video (optional): paste a YouTube link — students tap Watch the video and it plays inside the homework card.
8

Quizzes

  1. New quiz → title, map and pass mark.
  2. Add multiple-choice or true/false questions and mark the right answer.
  3. Tick Published → Save. For lets you aim it at one class.

Preview shows it as a student sees it; Results shows every score and which questions were hard.

Ten rewards for every letter

«مَنْ قَرَأَ حَرْفًا مِنْ كِتَابِ اللَّهِ فَلَهُ بِهِ حَسَنَةٌ، وَالْحَسَنَةُ بِعَشْرِ أَمْثَالِهَا»

“Whoever reads a letter from the Book of Allah gets a good deed, and each good deed is multiplied ten times.”

The Prophet ﷺ · Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 2910

«مَنْ دَلَّ عَلَى خَيْرٍ فَلَهُ مِثْلُ أَجْرِ فَاعِلِهِ»

“Whoever guides someone to good gets a reward like the one who does it.”

The Prophet ﷺ · Sahih Muslim 1893

And you share in it:

9

Attendance

  1. Pick the class and the date → Everyone present.
  2. Change anyone late, absent or excused → Save.

Saved sessions can be edited. Attendance appears in weekly reports and on the parent page.

10

Announcements & messages

  • Questions in announcement comments that wait for you are marked Needs reply.
  • 📨 Message a class or everyone sends the same note privately to each child.
  • You can edit your own messages, announcements and comments if you made a mistake.
11

Class files (PDFs)

  1. Open Teaching → 📁 Class files.
  2. Choose a PDF (up to 5 MB). The title fills in from the file name; add a note if you like.
  3. Pick For: one class, or every student → ⬆️ Upload & share.

Students see it under the map in From your teacher → 📁 Class files and tap ⬇️ Download. You can delete your own files at any time.

📦 Bigger than 5 MB? Save the PDF again with “Reduce file size”, or split it into two files.
12

Parents & weekly reports

  • Parent link: open a student → 👪 Parent link → copy it or send it on WhatsApp. Parents see progress, homework, attendance, badges, your notes, messages and your recitation feedback — read-only, no login. Reset link stops an old link working.
  • Weekly reports: 🖨️ on a student, a class or the Students tab → Print / Save as PDF.
  • Add students: create logins one by one or from a list (CSV).

A reward that never stops

«إِذَا مَاتَ الْإِنْسَانُ انْقَطَعَ عَنْهُ عَمَلُهُ إِلَّا مِنْ ثَلَاثَةٍ: إِلَّا مِنْ صَدَقَةٍ جَارِيَةٍ، أَوْ عِلْمٍ يُنْتَفَعُ بِهِ، أَوْ وَلَدٍ صَالِحٍ يَدْعُو لَهُ»

“When a person dies, their deeds end except for three: an ongoing charity, knowledge that benefits, or a righteous child who prays for them.”

The Prophet ﷺ · Sahih Muslim 1631

﴿إِنَّ الَّذِينَ يَتْلُونَ كِتَابَ اللَّهِ وَأَقَامُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَأَنفَقُوا مِمَّا رَزَقْنَاهُمْ سِرًّا وَعَلَانِيَةً يَرْجُونَ تِجَارَةً لَّن تَبُورَ﴾

“Indeed, those who recite the Book of Allah, establish prayer and spend from what We have provided them, secretly and publicly, hope for a trade that will never perish.”

Sūrah Fāṭir 35:29

The Tajweed you teach today will be recited by these children for the rest of their lives — and taught to their children. That is knowledge that keeps benefiting.

13

Questions

A student can’t see my homework, announcement or file. Check it is for their class (or “Every student”) and that they joined the class with the code.

A student isn’t in my Students list. Teachers only see students in their classes or accounts they created. Share your class code, or ask an admin to add them.

A student forgot their password. Open the student → Account → send a reset or set a new password.

Can I delete a student? No — only an admin can. You can disable an account in the student’s Account tab.

“Not allowed for your account role”. Your teacher account may not be approved yet, or the student isn’t linked to you. Ask an admin.

The teacher of good

«إِنَّ اللَّهَ وَمَلَائِكَتَهُ وَأَهْلَ السَّمَوَاتِ وَالْأَرَضِينَ حَتَّى النَّمْلَةَ فِي جُحْرِهَا وَحَتَّى الْحُوتَ لَيُصَلُّونَ عَلَى مُعَلِّمِ النَّاسِ الْخَيْرَ»

“Allah, His angels, and the inhabitants of the heavens and the earth — even the ant in its hole and even the fish — send blessings upon the one who teaches people good.”

The Prophet ﷺ · Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 2685

May Allah accept your teaching, put barakah in your time, and make these children a light for you on the Day you meet Him. 🤲

How classes work

  1. Create a class and you get a 6-letter class code.
  2. Give the code to your students — they type it in Tajweed Quest under “From your teacher → My class”. Or add them yourself here.
  3. When you post an announcement, quiz or message, choose the class it’s for. Other classes won’t see it.
  4. On the Students tab, pick a class to see only those students.

🚀 Quick start: your first day

  1. Add your full name. It is printed on certificates you sign and shown to students.
  2. Create your class. Classes → type a name (e.g. “Saturday Girls 8–10”) → Create class. You get a 6-letter class code.
  3. Bring students in. Give students the code (📋 Copy invite makes a message for parents), or tick students in Add students on the class page. New children: Add students tab creates their login.
  4. Set the first homework, e.g. “Memorise line 1” due next week.
  5. Take attendance each lesson.

☀️ Your weekly routine

Before class
  • Open Overview: “Needs your review”, “Waiting for your reply” and new recitations.
  • Answer messages and announcement questions.
In class
  • Attendance → Everyone present → change the few who are late or absent → Save.
  • Listen to Talqeen and approve finished maps (see below).
After class
  • Set homework for next week.
  • Post an announcement if needed.
  • Friday: print weekly reports for parents.

👩‍🎓 Students

  • Find a student: search by name, username, email or guardian; filter by class or status (ready for review, in progress, not started…).
  • Open a student card to see Profile, Progress, Maps & certificates, Quiz results and Account. You can add a photo, date of birth and guardian details.
  • Personal note & quiz: in Maps, write a note for one map and attach a personal quiz → Send note & quiz to student. It appears under that map for them only.
  • You only see your own students — children in your classes, or accounts you created. Anyone else joins with your class code.

✅ Approving a map (Talqeen check & certificate)

  1. A student finishes every lesson in a map and scores 12/15 or more in its Map Challenge → they appear in Overview → Needs your review.
  2. Tap Review →. Listen to the student recite every line of that map (Talqeen).
  3. Tick hifz and recitation, write feedback, sign with your finger or mouse, then Approve.
  4. The next map unlocks for the student and a certificate with your name is ready to print.

🏫 Classes

  • Each class has a join code. Students type it under From your teacher → Join your class.
  • From a class you can message it, announce to it, set its homework and print a class report.
  • 🏆 Class leaderboard: switch it on to let the class see each other’s streak, lines memorised and stars. Switch off any time.

📚 Homework

  • Types: Memorise lines (done at Memorise level 4) · Finish lessons · Pass a quiz · Record lines (students with Talqeen on) · Hand in a file (PDF, Word or PowerPoint — open See each student to download each file and give feedback) · Other task (the student ticks it).
  • Choose the lines, a due date and the class. The title fills itself in. Add a 🎬 YouTube link and it plays inside the homework for students.
  • Each card shows who is done, done late or late. 🔔 Remind sends a private message to everyone not done.

📝 Quizzes

  • New quiz → title, map, pass mark → add multiple-choice or true/false questions → mark the right answer → tick Published → Save.
  • For lets you aim it at one class. Personal quizzes only reach students you send them to from their Maps tab.
  • Preview shows it as a student sees it; Results shows every score and which questions were hard.

📋 Attendance

  • Pick the class and the date → Everyone present → change anyone late, absent or excused → Save.
  • Saved sessions can be edited or deleted. “Each student” shows attendance rates.
  • Attendance appears in weekly reports and on the parent page.

📣 Announcements & messages

  • Announcements show under a map (or all maps) for everyone or one class, with a comment section. Questions waiting for you are marked Needs reply.
  • Messages are private between you and one student. Message a class or everyone sends the same note to each child privately.

👪 Parents & reports

  • Parent link: open a student → 👪 Parent link → Copy or send on WhatsApp. Parents see progress, homework, attendance, badges, your notes and messages — read-only, no login. Reset link stops an old link working.
  • Reports: 🖨️ on a student, a class, or the Students tab → choose Full, Progress or Attendance report, the days (one day, from–to, or all days) and which marks to show (e.g. only present & absent) → Print / Save as PDF.

🎙️ Recitations & Talqeen recordings

  • From Memorise: every student can record a line in Memorise → 5. Record & listen and send it to you. It arrives in Overview → New recitations and in the student’s 🎙️ Talqeen tab. Play it, type feedback and Send — the child sees it under their recording, and parents see it in the parent report.
  • Talqeen (optional): off by default. Open a student → 🎙️ Talqeen → tick Talqeen on for this student to record lines for them to copy.
  • Record a line (up to 1 minute) → listen → Send. The student hears it under the map and can record themselves back; reply with feedback.

📁 Class files (PDFs)

  1. Open Teaching → Class files.
  2. Choose a PDF (up to 5 MB), give it a title and an optional note.
  3. Pick For: one class, or every student. Tap Upload & share.

Students see it under the map in From your teacher → “📁 Class files” and tap Download. You can delete your own files at any time.

✏️ Edit the course (teacher mode)

  1. Sign in to Tajweed Quest (the student app) with your teacher username — every map and lesson opens.
  2. Tap ✏️ Edit Map N to change a map’s name and its Map Challenge questions 1–14 (question 15 is the line match), or ✏️ Edit this lesson inside a lesson for its title, English meaning, explanation, easy explanation, Qur’an examples and Five Questions.
  3. Save for every student, or Reset to original to undo.

❓ Questions

A student says they can’t see my homework or announcement.

Check it is for their class (or “Every student”) and that they joined the class with the code. Class-only posts show only to members.

A student isn’t in my Students list.

Teachers only see students in their classes or accounts they created. Share your class code, or ask an admin to add them to your class.

A student forgot their password.

Open the student → Account → send a reset or set a new password. If they signed up with their own email, “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in page emails them.

Can I delete a student?

No — only an admin can. You can disable an account in the student’s Account tab.

“Not allowed for your account role”

Your teacher account may not be approved yet, or the student isn’t linked to you. Ask an admin.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a website in your browser. Similar browser technologies, such as local storage, can remember settings and progress on your device.

How Diin Islaam uses browser storage

  • Essential functions: sign-in sessions, security and features needed for an app to work.
  • Learning preferences: settings and progress may be kept on the device so you can continue later.
  • Advertising: on some general-audience pages, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads.

Google advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on those visits. You can manage personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also review Google's information about how it uses information from partner sites.

Your choices

You can delete or block cookies using your browser settings, although some sign-in or progress features may stop working. Where required, visitors may also be shown a consent message that allows advertising choices to be managed or withdrawn.

Children's areas

We avoid displaying Google advertising on the main child-focused Kids landing page. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of online services.

More information

See the Privacy Policy for information about accounts, course progress and data storage. Questions can be sent through the Contact page.

Contact us

Questions, corrections and suggestions are welcome.

How can we help?

You can contact Diin Islaam about:

  • a broken link, login issue or technical problem;
  • a correction to lesson text, Arabic, Afaan Oromoo or English;
  • course suggestions or accessibility feedback;
  • privacy, account or progress-data questions; or
  • community and teaching enquiries.

Email: fesbackups@gmail.com

Help us investigate quickly

Please include the page or course name, the web address if possible, what you expected to happen and what happened instead. Do not email passwords or other secret sign-in details.

Content corrections

For a Qur'an, hadith, translation or historical correction, include the exact sentence and the source you recommend checking. Submissions are reviewed before changes are made.

Privacy requests

For questions about an account or stored progress, use the email address above and describe the relevant course. We may need to confirm enough information to identify the correct record, but we will never ask you to send your password.

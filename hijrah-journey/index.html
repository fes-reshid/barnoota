MAKKAH

The Hijrah

622 CE · 1 AH · Traditional historical dating

More than fourteen centuries ago, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and his companion Abu Bakr (RA) made a courageous journey from Makkah toward Madinah. This journey is known as the Hijrah.

Today, our four explorers will travel through a learning adventure inspired by the places and lessons of the Hijrah.

This game presents a learning adventure, not a re-enactment — the Prophet ﷺ and Abu Bakr (RA) are never shown.

    How to Play

    • Up to four players ride together, each with their own camel and controls: Player 1 = WASD, Player 2 = Arrow Keys, Player 3 = IJKL, Player 4 = TFGH.
    • Explore each area and ride close to a glowing landmark to discover it — the whole group pauses together to learn something new.
    • Answer questions correctly to earn shared Journey Stars for the whole party.
    • Reciting or learning an ayah is never scored competitively — it's simply part of the journey.
    • Watch your camels' stamina — moving drains it, resting refills it.
    • There is no race and no single winner. Everyone who reaches Madinah completes the journey together.

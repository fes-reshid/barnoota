How it works

Install and choose your language

Download the installer and pick English, Arabic or Afaan Oromoo. No account needed.

Scan

NoorCleaner looks through your drives and shows what's using space, junk, duplicates and damaged files.

You decide

Choose what to remove: Quarantine, Recycle Bin or Delete. NoorCleaner always asks first.

What NoorCleaner does, honestly

  • Nothing is deleted unless you select it and confirm. Quarantined files can be restored.
  • NoorCleaner does not look for viruses. For virus protection use Windows Security, or our security program NoorGuard.
  • Recovering permanently deleted files is never guaranteed, especially on SSDs. The sooner you try, the better.
  • It is an early beta. Please try it yourself before relying on it, and tell us what you find.

Questions

Is NoorCleaner free?

Yes. There is no account, no subscription and no advertising inside the app.

Will it delete anything by itself?

No. It only lists what it finds. You choose what to remove, and it asks before every removal. The junk reminder only tells you; it never cleans by itself.

Does it find viruses?

No. NoorCleaner is about storage and damaged files. For virus scanning, network protection and security checks, use NoorGuard, our free security program.

Does it work in Arabic and Afaan Oromoo?

Yes. Choose the language when you install, or later in Settings. The translations are new, so corrections are welcome.

Can it recover files I deleted permanently?

Sometimes. It sets up Microsoft's free Windows File Recovery for you. It works best straight after deleting; on SSDs the data is often wiped quickly.

Does it send my files anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your PC, and it works without the internet.

How do I uninstall it?

Like any app: Windows Settings, then Apps, then NoorCleaner, then Uninstall.

How to use this book

🔊 Tap a speaker to hear each half of a line. 🎨 Every rule has its own colour: tap a colour at the top to show only that rule in the examples. 👆 Tap any Qur'an example to switch its colours off and on. 🔍 At the end of each lesson, find the rule in a full ayah. ✏️ In “Your turn”, choose the rule and the colours will appear. ✅ Tick each lesson when you can recite it. Your progress is saved on this device.

Tajweed is learned by listening and reciting to a qualified teacher. Use this book with your teacher.

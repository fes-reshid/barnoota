Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Diin Islaam ("we," "our," "the site") provides free Islamic educational content at diinislaam.com. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the site and its apps.

Information We Collect

If you create an account for the student portal, we collect your email address and progress data (lesson completion, quiz scores) needed to run the portal. We do not sell this information to anyone.

Cookies and Advertising

Some pages on this site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-Party Links

Some pages may contain links to third-party sites, including book retailers, where we may earn a small commission on qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Data Storage

Student portal data is stored securely using Google Firebase. Access is restricted so that each student can only view their own records.

Children's Privacy

Some of our content is used by children under adult/parental supervision (e.g. through a Quran school enrollment). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without a parent or guardian's involvement in account setup.

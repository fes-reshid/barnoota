Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Diin Islaam ("we", "our" or "the site") provides free Islamic educational content and interactive learning tools at diinislaam.com. This policy explains the information used by the website and its apps.

Information used for accounts

Students generally sign in with a username and password. Whether an email address is requested depends on the app. Arabic Adventure may request one for account recovery; the Qur'an Daily Tracker does not. Accounts created by an administrator for a class may have no student email address. Administrator accounts use an email address and password.

Firebase, which supports authentication for some courses, may keep an internal generated address in the form fesbackups+quest-username@gmail.com for a student account. This is generated from the username, is not requested from the student and delivers to the site administrator rather than to the student.

Learning progress

Depending on the course, we may store lesson completion, quiz scores, selected class and similar progress needed to provide the learning portal. Teachers or administrators connected to a class may be able to view student progress. We do not sell student progress information.

Qur'an Daily Tracker

The tracker can work without an account. When used without signing in, the learner's name, cycle start date and marked reading days stay in that browser on that device.

If a learner signs in, marked days may also be stored on the reminder server so the record can follow the learner between devices. That record uses an account identifier and may include marked days, cycle start date, time zone and reminder hour. The reminder record does not store the learner's name, username or email address.

Teacher visibility: teachers and administrators may see a class reading table showing whether a registered student marked a day as complete, and that table may be downloaded as a spreadsheet. Learners who do not want a reading record visible to a teacher should use the tracker without signing in.

Reminders remain off until enabled. If enabled, a browser notification address is stored so a reminder can be delivered. The tracker's “Delete my reminder data” control erases the reminder-server information described in this section without deleting the main course account.

Cookies, local storage and advertising

The website may use cookies and browser storage for essential sign-in, security, preferences and learning progress. Some general-audience pages may display advertisements served by Google AdSense.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on those visits. Visitors can manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings and can read how Google uses information from partner sites. If other third-party ad vendors are enabled, their technologies and opt-out choices may also apply.

Where required, visitors may be shown a consent message for advertising choices. See the separate Cookie Policy for more detail.

Child-focused pages

Some resources are intended for children to use with a parent, guardian or teacher. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information directly from children. The main Kids landing page does not load Google AdSense. Adults should supervise children's use and create or manage class accounts where appropriate.

Service providers

Google Firebase may support sign-in and progress storage. Cloudflare may support hosting, security or reminder functions. These providers process technical information needed to deliver their services under their own terms and privacy practices.

Third-party links and affiliate disclosure

The site may link to external websites, including reference sources, video platforms and book retailers. Some retail links may be affiliate links, meaning Diin Islaam may receive a small commission at no extra cost to the visitor. External websites control their own privacy practices.

Data choices and requests

Available choices depend on the app. They may include using a feature without signing in, turning reminders off, deleting reminder data, clearing browser storage or requesting help with an account record. To ask a privacy question or request assistance, email fesbackups@gmail.com. Do not send a password.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when features or service providers change. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page.

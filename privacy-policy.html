Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Diin Islaam ("we," "our," "the site") provides free Islamic educational content at diinislaam.com. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the site and its apps.

Information We Collect

Students sign in with a username and a password. Whether an email address is asked for depends on where the account is created: Arabic Adventure asks for one when you sign up, and keeps it so we can help you recover the account; the Qur'ān Daily Tracker does not ask for one at all. Accounts an admin creates for a class have no email address either. Admin accounts themselves use an email address and a password.

Separately, Firebase — which runs sign-in for us — keeps an internal address of the form fesbackups+quest-username@gmail.com against every student account. It is generated from the username, is not asked for, and delivers to us rather than to the student.

Alongside the account we keep the progress needed to run the portal: lesson completion, quiz scores, and which days a juz was marked as read. We do not sell this information to anyone.

Qur'ān Daily Tracker

The tracker works without an account. Used that way, your name, your cycle start date and the days you mark as read stay in your browser, on that device, and are not sent anywhere.

If you sign in — with the same username and password as the quest games — the days you mark as read are also kept on our reminder server, so they follow you between devices. That record holds only an account identifier, the days marked, your cycle start date, your time zone and the hour you chose to be reminded. Your name, your username and any email address are not stored there.

Teachers and admins can see it. The admin page shows every registered student against each day, with a tick where that day's juz was marked complete and a cross where it was not, and that table can be downloaded as a spreadsheet. If you would rather your reading not be visible to a teacher, use the tracker without signing in — everything except the reminders still works.

Reminders are off until you switch them on. If you do, we store the notification address your browser issues for that device, so a reminder can be delivered to it, and nothing else. You can switch them off at any time. Delete my reminder data, in the tracker's account panel, erases everything described in this section from the server; your account and your progress in the other apps are not affected.

The tracker's pages carry no advertising and no analytics.

Cookies and Advertising

Some pages on this site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-Party Links

Some pages may contain links to third-party sites, including book retailers, where we may earn a small commission on qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Data Storage

Student portal accounts and progress are stored using Google Firebase. The Qur'ān Daily Tracker's reading log and reminder settings are stored separately using Cloudflare, keyed only by an account identifier. Access is restricted so that each student can see only their own records; teachers and admins can additionally see the reading table described above.

Children's Privacy

Some of our content is used by children under adult/parental supervision (e.g. through a Quran school enrollment). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without a parent or guardian's involvement in account setup. Signing in needs only a username, accounts an admin creates for a class carry no email address, and the Qur'ān Daily Tracker never asks for one; where a sign-up does ask for an address it is kept only to help recover that account.

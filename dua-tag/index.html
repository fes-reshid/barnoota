Du'a Tag

A friendly 4-player tag game — get tagged, recite a du'a, stay safe!

← Back to Kids Corner Games

How to Play

  • One player is "It" and chases the others around the courtyard.
  • If It touches you, you're tagged and freeze in place. In Du'a Tag mode, a du'a (supplication) pops up on screen.
  • Read the Arabic, transliteration and English translation, then press "I Recited It".
  • You become the new "It", but you get 30 seconds of protection right after — nobody can tag you (and you can't tag anyone) while you catch your breath.
  • Classic Tag mode skips the du'a popup for a faster, simpler game. Practice Mode is untimed, just for getting comfortable with the du'as.
  • Controls: Player 1 = WASD, Player 2 = Arrow Keys, Player 3 = IJKL, Player 4 = TFGH. Game controllers also work if connected.
  • This is just a fun game — reciting a du'a here isn't a form of worship or a reward from Allah, it's simply how this game's "safe zone" works.
